Student Defense Checklist

How to protect yourself on WhatsApp, Discord, and Instagram

1. Check the Domain Core

Always look at the name right before .com or .org. If it says google.com.scam.tk, the actual site is scam.tk!

2. Look for Number Substitutions

Scammers swap letters with numbers to trick your eyes: 0 for O, 1 for l or i. (e.g. paypa1.com).

3. Beware Free Gift Claims

Links offering "Free Discord Nitro", "Free V-Bucks", or "Leaked Exam Papers" on non-official domains are almost 100% credential-harvesting scams.
